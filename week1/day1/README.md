1.What is CRM?  
*Customer Relationship Management (CRM) is a technology and strategy used by businesses to manage all interactions with current and potential customers. The primary goal is to improve business relationships by staying connected to customers, streamlining processes, and increasing profitability.
Instead of using disparate spreadsheets, a CRM provides a central "source of truth" where sales, marketing, and support teams can track every touchpoint a customer has with the brand.

## 3. Key Salesforce Objects Explained
{Account}
An Account represents an organization, company, or individual that you do business with. It is the parent record that holds the high-level information about a business entity (e.g., Company Name, Industry).
{Contact}
A Contact represents an individual person associated with an Account. While an Account is the company, the Contacts are the people you communicate with within that company (e.g., the CEO).
 {Opportunity}
An Opportunity represents a pending deal or a potential sale. It is used to track the progress of a business deal through various stages—from preparation to accomphlishment and includes data like the deal amount and expected close date.

🎓 Salesforce Basics: The College Map
If you're new to Salesforce, just imagine a University Campus.

🏛️ The Account = The Department
Think of: The Computer Science Dept or The Library.
The Job: It’s the Office/Building. It holds the address and the budget.
 Logic: The building stays there even after students graduate.

👤 The Contact = The Person
Think of: Pavan Ganesh (Student) or Dr. prakash (Professor).
The Job: This is the Individual. It holds their email and phone number.
 Logic: You can't talk to a building; you talk to the people inside it.

💼 The Opportunity = The Goal
Think of: Summer Internship or Research Grant.
The Job: This is the Potential Win. It tracks the money and the deadline.
 Logic: It’s a project that is either In Progress or Finished.
